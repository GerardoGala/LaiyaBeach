Mission Objective

Your ultimate goal is to finish the race course in the fastest time possible. To top the leaderboard, you cannot rely on luck; you must actively master and apply real-world ILCA sailing physics to maximize your speed.

How to Win

The timer starts the moment you click on "Start Simulation". Your performance depends entirely on how efficiently you navigate changing wind angles and optimize your boat trim.

1. Master VMG

  • Velocity Made Good: Do not just look at your boat speed.
  • True Progress: VMG measures how fast you travel directly toward the next mark.
  • The Tradeoff: Sailing directly toward a target might mean slower speeds, while sailing fast might take you far off course.

2. Perfect Your Trim

  • Sail Adjustments: Constantly adjust your boom angle, cunningham, and outhaul.
  • Point of Sail: Change your sail flatting and depth as your angle to the wind shifts.
  • Minimize Drag: Keep your sail shape perfectly aerodynamic for the current conditions.

3. Control Body Weight

  • Hiking Hard: Lean out to counteract high wind forces and keep the boat flat.
  • Boat Balance: A flat boat reduces rudder drag and creates optimal hydrodynamic lift underneath the hull.
  • Fore/Aft Trim: Shift weight to keep the bow from digging into waves or the stern from dragging.
