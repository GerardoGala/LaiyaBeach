ILCA Pro-Tip Strategy Manual

To master the leaderboard, you must know how Olympic-level sailors configure their boat trim. Optimize your 6 structural controls across all wind scales and headings to unlock maximum speed.

1. Upwind Tactics (Close Hauled)

Light Wind (0-7 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 3° to 8° (blocks split apart).
  • Sailor Position: Mid-Forward (near tank); lean leeward.
  • Daggerboard: Fully Down (2) for max tracking.
  • Vang: Slack; just taking out line play.
  • Cunningham: Loose; let loose wrinkles hold power.
  • Outhaul: Eased; hand-width gap to boom center.

Medium Wind (8-14 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 0° to 3° (block-to-block tight trim).
  • Sailor Position: Mid-Cockpit; hiking straight and hard.
  • Daggerboard: Fully Down (2) for strict tracking.
  • Vang: Firm; blocks boom rise in gusts.
  • Cunningham: Snug; pulled to erase luff folds.
  • Outhaul: Flat; two fingers gap away from boom.

Heavy Wind (15+ kn)

  • Boom Angle: 5° to 15° (play actively in gusts).
  • Sailor Position: Max Hike Out; hold boat flat.
  • Daggerboard: Up 2-3 inches; stops hull trip forces.
  • Vang: Max Tight; flattens middle of the sail.
  • Cunningham: Max Down; pulls sail draft forward.
  • Outhaul: Rigidly Flat; pulled tight to aluminum.

2. Reaching Tactics

Light Wind (0-7 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 35° to 65° (trim to telltales).
  • Sailor Position: Forward; clear out transom drag.
  • Daggerboard: Halfway Up (0) to shed friction.
  • Vang: Slack; lets boom lift up cleanly.
  • Cunningham: Completely off.
  • Outhaul: Well eased for a powerful driving pocket.

Medium Wind (8-14 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 35° to 65° (track target course path).
  • Sailor Position: Hiked out; shift aft on sudden waves.
  • Daggerboard: Halfway Up (0) for helm balance.
  • Vang: Set top batten parallel with boom.
  • Cunningham: Snugged slightly to lock shape profile.
  • Outhaul: Moderate depth for constant driving force.

Heavy Wind (15+ kn)

  • Boom Angle: 40° to 70° (dump fast if rolling over).
  • Sailor Position: Far Aft; keeps bow out of water.
  • Daggerboard: Up Significantly (-1) to drop drag.
  • Vang: Heavy tension; stabilizes sail dump.
  • Cunningham: Hard down; strips power from sail head.
  • Outhaul: Pulled flat; reduces high aerodynamic drag.

3. Downwind Tactics (Running)

Light Wind (0-7 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 75° to 85° (never push past 90°).
  • Sailor Position: Forward; heel hull to windward.
  • Daggerboard: Up High (-1) to minimize drag.
  • Vang: Eased; holds boom from dipping low.
  • Cunningham: Eased completely off.
  • Outhaul: Eased deep; broadens cross-section.

Medium Wind (8-14 kn)

  • Boom Angle: 90° to 100° (sail deep "by-the-lee").
  • Sailor Position: Mid-Cockpit; active surfing stance.
  • Daggerboard: Three-Quarters Up (-1.5) to clear drag.
  • Vang: Set leech open to twist with wind flow.
  • Cunningham: Eased completely off.
  • Outhaul: Eased slightly; smooth depth profile.

Heavy Wind (15+ kn)

  • Boom Angle: 90° to 110° (lock into deep by-the-lee).
  • Sailor Position: Transom Wall; ready to shift weight.
  • Daggerboard: Halfway Down (0) to act as stabilizer.
  • Vang: Tight; prevents high-speed death-rolls.
  • Cunningham: Pulled 1/4 down; cleans entry profile.
  • Outhaul: Pulled tight; stabilizes bottom panels.
