ILCA Simulator

Welcome to the world of Sailing ILCA Physics — where wind, trim, and heading converge to reveal the science behind speed and control.

This simulator is a training ground designed to show how every rig adjustment — from sheet tension to sailor position — transforms your Velocity Made Good (VMG) across each point of sail.

Experience the thrill of ILCA sailing with interactive controls. Every decision you make echoes the reality of the water, teaching you how physics drives performance.

At the end of the race, the simulator displays your telemetry data. Copy the data and submit it to an AI of your choice for analysis to help you improve your sailing strategy. See a sample analysis here.
