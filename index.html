ILCA Simulator


Welcome to the world of Sailing ILCA Physics — where wind, trim, and heading converge to reveal the science behind speed and control.

This simulator is a training ground designed to show how every rig adjustment — from sheet tension to sailor position — transforms your Velocity Made Good (VMG) across each point of sail.

Experience the thrill of ILCA sailing with interactive controls, live wind data, and a dynamic map view. Every decision you make echoes the reality of the water, teaching you how physics drives performance.
